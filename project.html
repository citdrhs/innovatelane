Welcome to InnovateLane.

InnovateLane is a communication platform designed by Center for Information Technology (CIT) students to allow individuals to form passion projects, create teams, and share ideas.

Why Join?

- Connect with like-minded individuals to build teams and create products of your choice!

- Build passion projects that align with your interests and even benefit society

- Colleges expect interests and passions

Who Should Join?

If you are an individual who is deeply passionate about STEM or simply want to find like-minded individuals, this platform is for you! Be ready to explore and be adventurous, because InnovateLane will be a cauldron of bustling ideas.
